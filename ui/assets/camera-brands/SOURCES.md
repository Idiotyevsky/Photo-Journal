# Camera wordmarks

`lumix.png` is the black LUMIX wordmark, rendered from the official vector
outlines on page 2 of Panasonic's LUMIX logo guideline. The transparent raster
preserves the original proportions; exports place it on a white background.

Source: https://lumix-base.jpn.panasonic.com/download/files/lumix_logo.pdf
Retrieved: 2026-10-15

The wordmark identifies the camera used for the photograph. It is not the
album application's logo. Camera brand names and marks belong to their owners.
Other recognized manufacturers currently use a plain text brand name.
