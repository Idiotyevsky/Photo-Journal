# Offline map data

[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/): public domain country outlines. Source: natural-earth-vector, ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson. Properties reduced and coordinates rounded to four decimals.

[GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/): cities15000 city gazetteer, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). City aliases filtered and reordered for offline city matching.

These maps illustrate travel footprints and are not road navigation.

## Vector illustrated maps

The active maps use real SVG paths from Natural Earth geographic data, pastel fills and SVG landscape decorations. There is no raster backdrop in either map. Route strokes, direction arrows, markers and labels retain their screen size while the map zooms; labels are repositioned for the visible viewport. Sources and processing details for vector-world.json and vector-china.json are documented in [vector-CREDITS.md](vector-CREDITS.md).
